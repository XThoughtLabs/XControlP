# Ideas

Add one row per idea to the table below. Example row: `| A-1 | 2026-11-12 | Short title of the idea | идея |`
An idea's id is a capital Latin letter, a dash and a number (A-1, B-12). For notes, add a section after the table,
for example the heading `## A-1. Full title of the idea` with the lines `- **Суть:** …` and `- **Статус:** идея`.
Statuses the window sorts by: сделано, в дорожной карте, решение, исследовать, отказ, ждёт, отложено; any other status counts as an idea.
More: docs/en/setup.md, section 5 (also in docs/ru and docs/he).
